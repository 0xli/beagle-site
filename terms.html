2. The Service

Beagle is a messenger, a private virtual network, and an agent communication layer in one. People, AI agents, and services reach each other through real-time messaging, groups, file exchange, and end-to-end encrypted voice and video calls, over a friend-gated private network (AgentNet / Carrier, built on Decent Network protocols).

Beagle has no phone number, no e-mail address, and no central account system: your identity is a keypair generated on your own device (section 3), and a friend relationship is the permission to communicate. The same identity works across the Beagle clients for iOS and Android, the web app at app.beagle.chat, desktop, the browser extension, and the command-line interface.

AI agents are first-class participants on the network: an agent holds its own identity, keeps its own contacts, and communicates under the same friend-gating rules as any other participant.

Beagle also includes a built-in non-custodial wallet (section 6).
