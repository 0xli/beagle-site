// what it does

Beagle Chat brings your Beagle identity into the browser.

Messages while the browser is open
Friends, requests and end-to-end encrypted chats, an unread badge, and a notification when no Beagle window is in front.
One identity everywhere
Create it here or import the key file from the desktop, the command line or app.beagle.chat — and export it the same way.
A profile you own
Your beagles.eth name and avatar, plus a private profile that stays on your device with a per-field disclosure policy.
Fill with Beagle
On a page with a form, press Fill. The Beagle Fill agent proposes answers from your own answer bank and profile, and quotes its price first. Private fields, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are never sent to it, and you always submit the form yourself.
Share the page you are on
Send the current tab to a friend in two clicks.
A Solana wallet as one tab
Create or import a wallet, send and receive, connect and sign for Solana dApps (Wallet Standard).

The same extension in Chrome and Firefox, with two differences.

Docked view
Chrome docks Beagle in the side panel. Firefox docks it in the sidebar.
macOS keychain
Saving wallet keys in the macOS keychain, through the optional helper, is Chrome-only. In Firefox they stay in the extension's own storage, encrypted under your password.

// what it keeps, and where

Your keys, messages and wallet stay on your device.

There is no account and no phone number, and relays only ever carry ciphertext. The one service of ours that receives page or profile content is the Fill agent, and only when you press Fill.

01Identity key

The keypair that is your Beagle identity — created on your device, in the extension's own storage, never sent anywhere.

02Friends and messages

Your roster, requests and history, on this device. End-to-end encrypted in transit.

03Private profile

Fields you fill in, each with a disclosure policy you set. Stored locally. Fields set to private or ask each time never leave the device; fields set to public or contacts may go to the Fill agent when you press Fill.

04Wallet keys

Seed phrases and private keys, encrypted under your password. Signing happens on the device.

Removing the extension removes it all — export your identity key and your wallet's recovery phrase first. The privacy policy says exactly what the Fill agent receives and how long it keeps it, and lists every network service the extension talks to. Privacy policy →
